Decoupler

1) Core Intent

Some of the Parts returned by LIST PARTS will be of type Decoupler . It is a type of Part , and therefore can use all the suffixes of Part . It serves as base for all decouplers, separators, launch clamps and docking ports. Most Decoupler parts are actually Separator parts, which is where most of the suffixes for them are found.
