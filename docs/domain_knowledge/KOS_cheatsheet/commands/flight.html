2) Safety Critical Notes

⚠ Warning: SAS OVERRIDES kOS However, SAS will tend to fight and/or override kOS’s attempts to steer. In order for kOS to be able to turn the ship, you need to set SAS OFF . You should take care in your scripts to manage the use of SAS appropriately. It is common for people writing kOS scripts to explicitly start them with a use of the SAS OFF command just in case you forgot to turn it off before running the script. You could also store the current state in a temporary variable, and re-set it at the conclusion of your script.
